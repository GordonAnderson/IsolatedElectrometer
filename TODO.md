## Possible existing bugs (not yet investigated)
- [x] **FIXED October 17, 2026** (`ADC.cpp`: the TC4 timer period is now computed from `TC4_CLOCK_HZ` = GCLK4 = 16 MHz, not `F_CPU` = 48 MHz). Before the fix the real sample rate was exactly 1/3 of `sampleRate` and `GSRATEA` reported the request. Measured from `STRIG` to `GTRIGCNT` incrementing over USB serial: 100 points at 10 kS/s took 30.9 ms (nominal 10), 1000 at 10 kS/s 301 ms (100), 1000 at 100 kS/s 31.2 ms (10): ratio 3.0 at every rate. After the fix the ratio is 1.00 to 1.02 from 10 to 100 kS/s and `GSRATEA` is correct. Note this changes behavior: a given `SSRATE` is now 3x faster than before, burst times are the nominal ones, and in mode 2 the `SFILTER` break frequency is now really what it says (it was 3x too high). The default `SSRATE` of 100000 was really 33 kS/s and is now a real 100 kS/s.
- [x] **LIMITED to 100000 October 31, 2026 (`rngSrate`), built, not flashed; MEASURED October 17, 2026, after the timer fix:** with the ADC clock at 750 kHz the ADC sustains **100 kS/s** (not the 53 kS/s the old comment claimed). `rngSrate` still allows up to 250000 Hz, and above 100 kHz requested trigger events are lost while a conversion is running: 120 to 200 kHz gives 2x the nominal burst time, 250 kHz gives 3x, and `GSRATEA` still reports the request. Sample values are correct (mean 3411.9, scatter under 0.1 count). To reach 250 kS/s raise the ADC clock (datasheet limit about 2.1 MHz, for example the 16 MHz GCLK4 divided by 8 = 2 MHz) and recheck noise at that rate; otherwise limit `rngSrate` to 100000.
- [x] Explained October 31, 2026: mode 2 divided the filtered sum by `averageSamples / 16` in integers, 62 instead of 62.5 for 1000 samples (55025 versus 54585 by calculation, bench 55022 versus 54577). Now `filteredSum * 16 / averageSamples`. Built, not flashed; bench check in BENCH_CHECKLIST.md.

- [x] October 31, 2026 (QT Py bench): sample buffers are one static 12 KB pool, not malloc (the allocator never shrank the heap, so buffers landed next to the stack and `safeMalloc` refused them; possibly the old SNUMP 1000 failure). `SNUMP` 10 to 5000, `SANUMS` 10 to 3000, refused with NAK outside that; a failed rebuild restores the last working settings.
